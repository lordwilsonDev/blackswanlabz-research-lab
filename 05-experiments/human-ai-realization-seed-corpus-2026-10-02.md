# Human–AI Idea Realization Seed Corpus — 2026-10-02

## Purpose

A retrospective corpus of ten historical idea-to-artifact episodes for the Human–AI Idea Realization framework.

These are not scored results.

They are candidate frozen cases from the existing artifact trail.

## Case structure

Each case records:

- idea/artifact
- earliest identifiable specification
- later realized artifact
- verification state
- reuse/accumulation signal
- attribution limitations.

A historical case cannot by itself identify whether improvement came from the human, model, tools, memory, or process.

## What this corpus is for

The ten cases are suitable for a controlled follow-up because each can be converted into a frozen intent representation without requiring the historical interaction log to be perfectly reconstructed.

The next step is not to score the past.

The next step is to sample a subset of these ideas and replay them under controlled model conditions.

## Proposed replay matrix

For each selected idea:

| Factor | Levels |
|---|---|
| Intent | Raw / Structured / Executable |
| Model | Model A / Model B / Model C / … |
| Harness | Direct / Standardized |
| Evaluation | First-pass / Final / Verified / Transfer |

## Primary outputs

- intent-to-artifact fidelity
- first-pass realization
- omission rate
- repair burden
- verification closure
- transfer
- realization cost
- retained capability

## Historical baseline

The original realized artifact becomes a **historical reference**, not a gold-standard answer.

The comparison asks:

> Can another human–AI system realize the same underlying idea to comparable or better verified fidelity under the same registered intent contract?

That is the scientifically useful comparison.

## Core warning

The historical artifact may contain unrecorded human intervention.

Therefore:

**historical quality ≠ historical first-pass capability**

and

**repository quality ≠ prompting quality**.

This corpus is deliberately designed to preserve that distinction.
