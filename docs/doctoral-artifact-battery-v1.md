## Purpose

This is the next-stage artifact evaluation protocol for an already-existing body of cross-domain work.

The experiment is **not starting from zero**.

The participant has already produced a substantial public/private repository record spanning software engineering, AI systems, verification, formal methods, research methodology, automation, security/governance, language/question engineering, business systems, and other domains. # 4. What "Doctoral-Level Artifact" Means Here

"Doctoral-level" is an **evaluation target**, not an honorary title.

An artifact qualifies for a doctoral-level designation only when its requirements, methodology, technical depth, originality where required, evidence, and independent verification support that designation.

Therefore:

> **The repository may contain doctoral-level artifacts before this experiment begins; the evaluation determines which artifacts actually satisfy the standard.**

Do not downgrade an artifact merely because it was created outside a university.

Do not upgrade an artifact merely because it is impressive.

Judge the artifact.

# 9. Capability Scoring

Level 0 — Claim  
Assertion without substantial artifact.

Level 1 — Demonstration  
Task performance with substantial assistance.

Level 2 — Construction  
Functioning artifact.

Level 3 — Verification  
Demonstrates satisfaction of explicit requirements.

Level 4 — Research  
Original, defensible investigation.

Level 5 — Independent research  
Qualified external inspection/reproduction/evaluation.

Level 6 — Contribution  
Defensible useful or novel contribution relative to the field.

Existing artifacts may begin above Level 0. The purpose of evaluation is to determine their evidence-supported level.

# 14. Experimental Discipline

Do not rebuild work that already exists merely to make the story cleaner.

Instead:

1. inventory existing evidence
2. classify existing artifacts
3. evaluate their evidence
4. identify genuine gaps
5. design new artifacts only where needed
6. freeze requirements
7. construct
8. test
9. independently evaluate
10. update the evidence map

The experiment is cumulative.
