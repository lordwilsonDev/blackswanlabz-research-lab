# 0. THE NORTH STAR

## BlackSwanLabz is a trusted business intelligence and modernization partner

BlackSwanLabz helps companies understand their changing environment, discover opportunities, test new technology, and continuously improve—using AI as the underlying leverage, not as the thing being sold.

**Category:** AI Operations Intelligence & Automation Firm

**Delivery model:** Remote Business Operations Intelligence + AI Orchestration + Automation Management

**Relationship type:** Continuously maintained business intelligence + automation relationship

**Core promise:**

> **We help businesses keep up with change.**

We research your business, your customers, your competitors, your industry, and the technology available to you. We identify what matters, explore it with you, and help test and implement improvements when they make sense.

> **We don't automate for the sake of automation. We prove value first.**

**Durable layer:** Research + Business DNA + Evidence + Human Context + Decision-making + Orchestration + Verification + Measurement + Institutional Memory.

The technology underneath can be swapped.

**Stay-current promise:** You don't have to keep up with everything happening in technology. That's part of what we're here for.

# 1. BLACKSWANLABZ THESIS

BlackSwanLabz is an external intelligence and automation operation.

We research businesses using public and authorized information, identify operational opportunities, validate those opportunities with the client, implement appropriate improvements or automations, measure outcomes, and continuously monitor the business for new opportunities and risks.

### Core promise

> **Research your business. Find what matters. Give you options. Build what you approve. Measure what changed. Keep watching.**

### Core rule

> **Never confuse finding an opportunity with proving an opportunity.**

And:

> **Never confuse proving an opportunity with delivering measurable improvement.**

# 2. DESIGN PRINCIPLE: END-FIRST ENGINEERING

Every BlackSwanLabz process begins with the desired end state.

For every proposed activity ask:

1. What outcome are we trying to produce?
2. What evidence proves the outcome occurred?
3. What information was required to produce it?
4. What decision had to occur before it?
5. What process generated that decision?
6. What system supports that process?
7. What human approval is required?
8. What can fail?
9. How do we detect failure?
10. How do we recover?

This prevents building impressive infrastructure that doesn't produce business value.

# 3. CLIENT LIFECYCLE

## Operating principle

BlackSwanLabz operates as a remote-first, AI-native intelligence and automation firm.

We do not rebuild client software stacks. We integrate with the client's existing ecosystem: CRM, Email, Documents, Calendar, Chat, ERP, and their existing AI subscriptions.

Progressive access governs every engagement:

```text
Level 0 — Public Research
  website / competitors / reviews / hiring / public filings
Level 1 — Interview
  what happens / who does it / where stuck / systems used
Level 2 — Read-Only Access
  CRM / Analytics / Documents / Reports
Level 3 — Limited Write
  draft / task / record / notification creation
Level 4 — Production Automation
  test → approval → deployment → monitoring
```

Access is granted incrementally based on trust, evidence quality, and demonstrated value.

## Customer file as central operational record

Every client has a dedicated customer file with:

```text
client_control.yaml front door
structured operational store
human-readable file store
event ledger core
```

The customer file supports full reconstruction of the client relationship from first research through current state.

The filesystem is not used as a database. The customer file is not merely a folder containing a PDF.

## Stack-orchestration mandate

Automations must connect to existing client software via OpenAI, Claude, or client-managed AI subscriptions.

Building from scratch is acceptable only when no existing system can be orchestrated.

# 7. EVIDENCE MODEL

Every finding receives an evidence classification.

```text
OBSERVED
DOCUMENTED
DECLARED
INFERRED
HYPOTHESIS
UNKNOWN
```

### OBSERVED

Directly observed public evidence.

### DOCUMENTED

Explicitly documented by the company or authoritative source.

### DECLARED

Statement made by leadership/company.

### INFERRED

Reasonable interpretation of evidence.

### HYPOTHESIS

Possible explanation requiring validation.

### UNKNOWN

Insufficient evidence.

Never present an inference as a fact.
