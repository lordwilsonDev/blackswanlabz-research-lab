# BlackSwanLabz onboarding

Procedure, not evidence. Read [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) first: only `verified` rows in [CLAIMS.md](../../CLAIMS.md) are established.

1. **Discover before you build.** Read `README.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `llms.txt`, `CLAIMS.md`, `resources/README.md`. List the skills and harnesses you can actually find, with exact paths. Class each as executable, documented, design only, or not found in the scope you searched. Say what you searched. Never call a design an installed skill.
2. **Ask the 50 questions** in `08-operations/50-question-os-bootstrap-v1.md`, one at a time, in order. Accept YES, NO or UNKNOWN. Record each answer with a one-line purpose. A NO is information. Do not invent answers.
3. **Draft scope from the lab, not from a vote.** Inside scope = the client loop in `08-operations/complete-client-loop.md` as updated in `08-operations/README.md`. Outside scope = anything the "What exists" table in `README.md` marks designed or not built. Mark the draft PROPOSED. The owner approves.
4. **Compile** after the 50: state, capabilities, gaps, risks, roles, SOPs, KPIs and OKRs (provisional until a baseline and data source exist), approvals needed, and the smallest next task.
5. **Close the loop.** After any authorized task, compare what happened with what was approved, keep the evidence, and ask the next question.

## How to work with the owner

- The owner's stated decisions are decisions. Record them as decisions. Do not re-argue them.
- When a document disagrees with a decision, add a dated update note and leave the original text as written. Say what you changed. Fix only what would mislead a reader.
- Draft, then ask. Consequential actions (money, people, publishing) need explicit approval. Do not claim anything ran that did not.
